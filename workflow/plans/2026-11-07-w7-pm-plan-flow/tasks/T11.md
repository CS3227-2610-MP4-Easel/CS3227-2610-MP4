## Objective
The five endpoints work through the policy gate. `getPmRuntime()` builds the flow lazily, registers the context source once, and `LLM_MODE=mock` completes create, expand and approve against the database.

## Rules that apply
- AD-55 - routes carry no role checks; the gate does it. The route layer is thin: parse, call the flow, map errors. `src/be/api` imports no database client.
- Body limits: endpoint 34 at most 32 768 bytes, endpoints 37, 40, 41 at most 1 024 bytes; check `Content-Length`, then count streamed bytes, before parsing (`before` hook, 413 `PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE`). Errors use `errorResponse`/`HttpError`; `Retry-After` header on 429.
- Archived project: the filtered client hides proposals, so endpoints 35, 37, 40, 41 answer 404 (a recorded Deviation).
- Until W5 exists the placeholder `requireMember` throws `NOT_FOUND`; route tests inject their own `AuthDependencies` (see `src/be/api/test/register-route.test.ts`) with the proposal resolver from T5 and a membership check.
