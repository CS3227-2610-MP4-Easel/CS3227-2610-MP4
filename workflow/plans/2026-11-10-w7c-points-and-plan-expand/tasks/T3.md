## Objective
`FeatureConfig` accepts optional `attemptTimeoutMs`, `deadlineMs`, `leaseMs`; the gateway uses them over `LIMITS` for that feature's attempts and `IN_FLIGHT` lease; PM_PLAN_EXPAND is set to 54,000 / 54,000 / 75,000 with `maxTokens` 4,200 and `maxPromptTokens` 7,000; every other feature behaves exactly as today.

## Rules that apply
- A timeout or other non-HTTP error never retries; a 429 (after `backoff429Ms`) or a 5xx or network error retries once when `deadline - elapsed - wait >= minRetryMs`; the retry's timeout is the time left. This logic is unchanged; only the numbers become per feature. `minRetryMs` and `backoff429Ms` stay global.
- The spend reservation (`reservation()` in `budget.ts`) already reads `FEATURES[feature]`; do not edit `budget.ts`.
- No other feature's value changes.
