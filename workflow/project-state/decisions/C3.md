# C3 — Each slice owner approves their own specs, plans and branch reviews

- **Date:** 2026-10-31
- **Who asked:** operator (the team), configured by Claude Sonnet 5.5
- **Supersedes:** —
- **Superseded by:** —
- **Shapes area:** —
- **Binding (guardrails.md):** no

## Decision

The `Approved:` field is written by the slice owner of that branch's workstream, and by a teammate for work they cross-review. Fold is run by whoever merges, on their yes in chat.

## Why

Three members work in parallel on role slices (AD-72); a single approver would be the bottleneck before the 13 Nov deadline. Security work is cross-reviewed (conventions/process.md).

## Rejected Alternatives

- One named owner approves everything.
- Leave approver generic.
