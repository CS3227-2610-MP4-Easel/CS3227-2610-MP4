# C34 — Permit an alternate security reviewer when `@ludannnn` authors a sensitive PR

- **Date:** 2026-11-05.
- **Who asked:** operator (Dev Lead), reversing C31 for the `@ludannnn` author case.
- **Decision:** For a sensitive PR authored by `@ludannnn`, `@teleifo` and `@Xiao-Congchen` may fill the security-reviewer and human-approver roles in either order, provided they are distinct and neither is the PR author. Retain C31's mapping for `@teleifo` authors and other authors.
- **Why:** The security reviewer cannot approve their own PR, so the two remaining trusted people must be eligible to satisfy the two separate roles.
